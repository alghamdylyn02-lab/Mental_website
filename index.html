CONQUERING DEPRESSION
  • Depression is a mood disorder that causes a persistent feeling of sadness and loss of interest.
  • Symptoms of depression may include sadness, irritability, clinginess, worry, aches and pains, refusing to go to school, or being underweight.
  • Think back to when you feel fairly sure you weren't being affected by depression. Try writing your answers down if it helps
If you feel depressed, make an appointment to see your doctor or mental health professional as soon as you can.
Overcoming anxiety
  • Anxiety is a feeling of fear, dread, and uneasiness.
  • Deep breathing exercises, progressive muscle relaxation, and mindfulness meditation can help calm your nervous system and reduce anxiety levels.
  • Stay active. Participate in activities that you enjoy and that make you feel good about yourself.
  • Enjoy social interaction and caring relationships, which can lessen your worries.

TIPS FOR GOOD SLEEP
  • Develop a calming bedtime routine to signal to your body that it's time to wind down.
  • Aim to avoid electronic devices such as smartphones, computers, and TVs for at least an hour before bedtime.
  • Avoid heavy meals, caffeine, and alcohol close to bedtime, as these can disrupt sleep. Instead, opt for light snacks if you're hungry, and stay hydrated throughout the day.
By prioritizing good sleep hygiene and making small changes to your sleep habits, you can support your mental health and overall well-being.
